# Getting Started with Contoso Global Solutions Lab

## Welcome!
Welcome to the **Multi-Region Deployment Training** lab. This lab is designed to help you understand Azure multi-region deployment concepts.

## Lab Environment
Your lab environment has been pre-configured with the following:

| Resource | Details |
|---|---|
| Resource Group 2 | Sandbox — create and manage resources here |
| Resource Group 3 | Pre-deployed Storage Account |
| Lab Duration | 14 Days |
| Attempts | 1 (cannot be restarted once expired) |

## Prerequisites
- Access to Microsoft Azure Portal
- Your lab credentials are shown in the **Environment** tab on the left

## How to Access Azure Portal
1. Click on the **Environment** tab on the left panel
2. Copy your **Azure Username** and **Password**
3. Open [https://portal.azure.com](https://portal.azure.com)
4. Login with the credentials provided
5. You are now ready to begin the exercises!

## Connect to Jump VM
1. Click the **Connect** button on this lab page
2. Choose **Connect via Browser** for web-based RDP
3. Enter VM credentials when prompted
4. The Jump VM will open in a new browser tab

> **Important:** You have only **1 attempt** for this lab. Please do not delete your resource groups.
